## Deviations from the WO, and why

- **Phase 3b run out of order**, before Phases 1–2 completed. The maintainer was
  positioned at a cutscene and the opportunity was transient; phases are
  explicitly independent.
- **The cutscene watcher was rewritten before use.** The first version stopped
  after one `acted=1` edge, which would have fired on a dialogue-camera scene and
  gone deaf before the real cutscene. Caught because the maintainer volunteered
  that a long dialogue came first.
- **Phase 1 was not fired.** Step 0 refused it. Stated as a refusal, not a
  failure, and not worked around.
